Guest access allows external parties — investors, lawyers, auditors, and other reviewers — to view documents without creating a full ArgoMedix VDR account. They access the room through a secure link and can be required to read and accept a non-disclosure agreement before any document is shown. This keeps your deal workflow frictionless while maintaining a legally defensible audit trail.

## Enabling Guest Access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Access settings">
    Navigate to your data room, then go to **Settings → Access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Guest Access on">
    Flip the **Enable Guest Access** toggle to the on position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an access expiry date (optional)">
    Use the **Guest Access Expiry** date picker to automatically disable guest access after a specific date. This is useful for deal timelines with hard closing deadlines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable NDA Acceptance (optional)">
    If you want guests to acknowledge a non-disclosure agreement before viewing documents, toggle **Require NDA Acceptance**. See the [NDA Acknowledgment Workflow](#nda-acknowledgment-workflow) section below for configuration steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save**. Guest access is now active and a shareable link becomes available under **Users → Share Link**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## NDA Acknowledgment Workflow

When **Require NDA Acceptance** is enabled, guests encounter a full-screen NDA gate immediately after following the access link. They cannot view or interact with any document until they accept.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the NDA gate">
    In **Settings → Access**, toggle **Require NDA Acceptance** to on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide your NDA document">
    Choose one of two options:
    - **Upload a PDF** — click **Upload NDA Document** and select a PDF from your computer. The PDF is displayed in the built-in viewer on the acceptance screen.
    - **Use the built-in template** — click **Use Built-in Template** to populate a standard mutual NDA pre-filled with your organization's name and the current date. You can edit the template text before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Guests read and accept">
    When a guest opens the link, they are presented with the NDA document and an **Accept** button. They must scroll to the end of the document before the button becomes active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acceptance is recorded">
    Upon clicking **Accept**, the guest's name, email address, IP address, and a precise timestamp are written to the data room's **Audit Trail**. The guest is then granted access to the document index.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  NDA acceptances are legally recorded in the audit trail with the guest's name, email, IP address, and timestamp. Download the audit trail from **Settings → Audit Trail → Export** to retain records for your legal files.
</Note>

## Shareable Access Links

Rather than sending individual email invitations, you can generate a single shareable link that any guest can use to enter the data room.

**To generate a shareable link:**

1. Go to **Users → Share Link** inside your data room.
2. Configure the link options:
   - **Link Expiry** — set a date after which the link becomes inactive.
   - **Require Email Verification** — when enabled, guests must verify their email address before gaining access. This ensures you can identify each person who uses the link even if it is forwarded.
3. Click **Generate Link** and copy the URL to share it via email, messaging, or your deal platform.

You can revoke a shareable link at any time from **Users → Share Link → Revoke Link**. Revoking the link immediately prevents new guests from entering, but does not terminate sessions that are already active.

<Warning>
  Anyone with the link can access the data room (subject to NDA if enabled). Treat shareable links like passwords — do not post them in public channels, shared inboxes, or unsecured communications.
</Warning>
