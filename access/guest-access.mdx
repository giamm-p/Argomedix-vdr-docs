Argomedix VDR lets you share your data room with external investors through secure share links. Each guest must register with their name and email, verify with a one-time password sent by email, and then browse only the folders and documents their group is allowed to see.

## Opening a share link

When you create a group and generate a share link, the guest receives a URL like `https://app.argomedix.com/share/<hash>`. If the link has an expiry date and that date has passed, or if the link was deactivated, the guest sees a message that the link is invalid or expired.

## Guest registration

The first time a guest opens the link, they see a registration screen titled with your workspace name, for example, `AcmeCorp Data Room`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Type your **Full Name** and **Email Address**, then click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the OTP">
    Argomedix sends a 6-digit verification code to the email address. The email subject is `Your verification code for <Workspace Name>` and the code is valid for 15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Type the 6-digit code into the **Verification Code** field and click **Verify Code**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the code is wrong, the guest sees how many attempts remain. After 5 failed attempts on the same code, they must request a new one. Guests can also click **Resend Code** after a 60-second cooldown, or click **Change Email** to start over with a different address.

<Note>
  To prevent abuse, no more than 3 OTPs can be requested for the same email within any 10-minute window.
</Note>

## Guest session and timeouts

Once verified, the guest is logged in and a session cookie is set for that specific share link. The session has two limits:

- **Absolute maximum:** 12 hours from the moment of login, even if the guest stays active.
- **Idle timeout:** 30 minutes of inactivity.

The app monitors mouse movement, keyboard input, clicks, and scrolling. If the guest is idle for 27 minutes, a modal appears with the title **Session Expiring Soon** and the message that the session will expire in 3 minutes. The guest must click **Keep Me Signed In** to reset the idle timer. If they do nothing for 3 more minutes, the page reloads and the guest must verify again.

Behind the scenes, the session is refreshed every 5 minutes while the guest is active and before the warning threshold.

## What guests see in the data room

After login, the guest sees the same folder structure you have configured, but only the folders and documents their group has permission to view or download. Hidden items are completely omitted from the tree and document list.

The guest view includes:

- **Data Room Index:** a collapsible folder tree on the left (or in a mobile drawer).
- **Search bar:** a global search field at the top labeled **Search in Data Room...** that supports OCR-powered full-text search across allowed documents.
- **Document list:** files in the selected folder, shown with their file-type icon and display name.
- **Workspace logo:** if you have enabled logo display in workspace settings, the company logo appears in the top-left corner.

Guests can click any document to open it in a secure viewer. Supported formats are PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, PNG, JPG, GIF, WEBP, SVG, MP4, WEBM, DICOM (`.dcm`), and CAD files (`.stl`, `.obj`). STEP and IGES files are not viewable in the browser; the guest is prompted to download the original file instead.

## Downloads

Guests can download a document only when all of the following are true:

1. The group permission for that document is set to **Download**.
2. The workspace-wide setting **Watermark on Files** is set to **Original files (No Watermark)**.

If watermarks are enabled for the workspace, downloads are disabled entirely and the guest sees a message that downloads are disabled for this file. When downloads are allowed, the file is served as an attachment with its original filename.

## Search as a guest

Guests can search inside the data room using the same search bar as admins. Results are filtered to documents the guest is allowed to see. Each result shows the file name and a text snippet with the matching term highlighted. Clicking a result opens the document directly. Guests can also click **View detailed results** to see the full search results page at `/share/<hash>/search`.

## Removing guest access

Admins can remove a guest at any time from the **Permissions** page under the **Guests** tab. When you remove a guest:

- Their access is revoked immediately.
- They can no longer log in or view documents.
- Their activity history is preserved for auditing.

If the guest was invited by a different admin, that original inviter receives an in-app notification that their guest was removed.

## What guests cannot do

Guests are read-only participants. They cannot:

- Upload, move, rename, or delete folders or documents.
- Change permissions or group settings.
- View hidden folders or documents.
- Access the audit log, analytics, or team management pages.
- See workspace settings or billing information.
- Create or manage share links.
- Access documents outside the folder tree assigned to their group.
