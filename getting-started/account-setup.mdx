Argomedix VDR is currently in private preview and registration is invite-only. You can create an account by redeeming an invite code, requesting one through the waitlist, or accepting a team invitation. This page explains each path from start to finish.

| Goal | Address |
| --- | --- |
| Sign up as a new user | [app.argomedix.com/welcome](https://app.argomedix.com/welcome) |
| Activate an invite code | [app.argomedix.com/activate](https://app.argomedix.com/activate) |
| Sign in as an existing user | [app.argomedix.com/login](https://app.argomedix.com/login) |

## Request an invite code

If you do not have an invite code, you can join the waitlist on the [welcome page](https://app.argomedix.com/welcome).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure your data room">
    On the welcome page, choose your industry. Select **MedTech** to access diagnostic profiling and tailored folder templates, or **Other industry** for a universal template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your folder structure">
    Review the suggested folders and placeholder documents. You can choose **Use this folder structure** or **Start with a blank data room**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Provide your full name, work email, and company name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request access">
    Click **Request Code** and answer the prompt: "What is the #1 reason you need a data room?"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    A 6-digit verification code is sent to your email. Enter it within 10 minutes to confirm your request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After verification, your request is submitted for review. When approved, you receive your invite code at the same email address. Rate limits apply: you can request a new code once every 5 minutes, up to 2 times per hour, and up to 3 times per 24 hours.

## Sign up with an invite code

Once you have a valid invite code, return to the [welcome page](https://app.argomedix.com/welcome) and select **Access Data Room**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your invite code">
    Type the code exactly as received. Codes are case-insensitive and automatically converted to uppercase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your sign-up method">
    You can sign up with **Google** or with **email and password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete email verification">
    If signing up by email, enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your inbox. The code expires after 10 minutes. You can resend it once if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your password">
    Your password must be at least 8 characters and include at least 1 number and 1 special character.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After successful verification, your workspace is created automatically and you are redirected to your data room.

## Log in

Go to [app.argomedix.com/login](https://app.argomedix.com/login) and sign in with your email and password, or use **Continue with Google**.

If you enter incorrect credentials too many times, the system enforces a 10-minute rate limit on login attempts from your IP address or email.

## Reset your password

If you forgot your password, click **Forgot Password?** on the login page. Enter your email address and the system sends a reset link. Follow the link to set a new password that meets the same requirements (minimum 8 characters, 1 number, 1 special character). After updating, you are redirected to the login page.

<Note>
  Password reset is only available for email-based accounts. If you signed up with Google, use Google's account recovery instead.
</Note>

## Join a team via invite link

Workspace owners and admins can invite team members by email. When you receive an invitation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the invite link">
    The link contains a unique token and opens the sign-up or login flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign up or log in">
    If you do not have an account, you are prompted to create one with the invited email. If you already have an account, log in to join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invitation">
    Once authenticated, you are added to the workspace automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Team invitations expire after 24 hours for security. If your invitation expires, ask the workspace owner or admin to send a new one.

## Activate an existing data room

If you already configured a data room during the waitlist process and later received an invite code, use the [activate page](https://app.argomedix.com/activate). Enter your email and invite code to retrieve your previously saved onboarding profile and launch your workspace.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Invalid invite code" | Check for typos. Codes expire and have usage limits. Request a new code if needed. |
| "User already registered" | Log in instead of signing up. If you forgot your password, use the reset flow. |
| "Too many attempts" | Wait 10 minutes before trying again. |
| "Verification code expired" | Request a new 6-digit code. You can resend once per attempt. |
| "No data room associated with this email" | Complete the welcome flow first to configure your workspace, then activate with your code. |

For additional help, contact [support@argomedix.com](mailto:support@argomedix.com).
