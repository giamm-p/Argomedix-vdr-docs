Setting up your account is the foundation for all data room activity in ArgoMedix VDR. Before you can create data rooms, invite collaborators, or manage documents, you need a verified account tied to your organization's workspace. This guide walks you through every step — from registration to enabling two-factor authentication — so your environment is secure and ready for deal workflows.

## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the ArgoMedix VDR sign-up page">
    Navigate to [app.argomedix.com](https://app.argomedix.com) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Get Started">
    On the homepage, click the **Get Started** button to open the registration form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your account details">
    Enter your **full name**, **work email address**, and a strong **password**. ArgoMedix VDR requires passwords to be at least 12 characters and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy">
    Review and accept the [Terms of Service](https://argomedix.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://argomedix.com/privacy) by checking the confirmation box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from **noreply@argomedix.com**. Click the **Verify Email** link inside to activate your account. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend Verification Email** on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Your Organization

After verifying your email, ArgoMedix VDR prompts you to configure your organization workspace. This workspace is the shared environment your entire team operates within.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your organization name">
    Type your company or firm name exactly as you want it to appear across the platform and in any exported audit reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your industry">
    Select the industry that best describes your organization from the dropdown:

    - **Life Sciences**
    - **Healthcare**
    - **Finance**
    - **Legal**
    - **Other**

    Your selection tailors default templates and compliance-related prompts to your sector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your organization logo (optional)">
    Click **Upload Logo** and select a PNG or SVG file (recommended size: 200×60 px). Your logo appears in the workspace header and on data room access pages visible to invited reviewers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your timezone">
    Choose your local timezone from the list. ArgoMedix VDR uses this setting to display activity timestamps, schedule automated notifications, and generate audit logs in a consistent time reference.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profile Settings

You can update your personal profile at any time by clicking your **avatar** in the top-right corner of the dashboard and selecting **Profile** from the dropdown menu.

From your profile page you can configure the following:

- **Display name** — the name shown to other workspace members and reviewers inside data rooms.
- **Profile photo** — upload a headshot or team avatar (JPG or PNG, max 5 MB).
- **Notification preferences** — choose which email alerts you receive, including document activity, new reviewer logins, and deal status updates.
- **Two-factor authentication** — enable an additional layer of login security (see the section below for step-by-step instructions).

## Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second verification step each time you log in, protecting your account even if your password is compromised.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Security** from your account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Two-Factor Authentication">
    Toggle the **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** switch to the on position. A QR code setup dialog will appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open your authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator) and scan the QR code displayed on screen. This links your app to your ArgoMedix VDR account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the 6-digit confirmation code">
    Type the 6-digit time-based code generated by your authenticator app into the confirmation field and click **Verify**. 2FA is now active on your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  We recommend enabling 2FA for all accounts, especially administrators who control data room access.
</Note>

## Inviting Team Members

Once your organization workspace is configured, workspace admins can invite colleagues directly from **Settings → Team**. You can assign roles such as Admin, Manager, or Member to control what each person can see and do across the workspace.

For complete instructions on managing roles, sending invitations, and revoking access, see [Inviting Users](/access/inviting-users).
