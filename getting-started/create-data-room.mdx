A data room in ArgoMedix VDR is a secure, isolated workspace that lets you share sensitive documents with a defined group of reviewers under controlled conditions. Each data room operates independently — with its own access list, permissions, audit trail, and security settings — so you can run multiple deals or review processes simultaneously without any overlap. Common use cases include M&A due diligence, Series A and growth-stage fundraising, regulatory submissions, and clinical trial data sharing with sponsors or CROs.

## Create a New Data Room

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Data Room dialog">
    From your **Dashboard**, click the **+ New Data Room** button in the top-right corner of the data rooms panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a Data Room Name">
    Type a clear, descriptive name for the data room. For sensitive deals, consider using a codename to avoid disclosing details prematurely — for example, **"Project Falcon – Series A"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Description (optional but recommended)">
    Enter a brief description to give your team context about the deal or purpose. This description is visible only to workspace members, not to external reviewers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Data Room Type">
    Select the security tier that matches your deal's requirements:

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Standard" icon="folder-open">
        Full document access for reviewers, including the ability to download permitted files and copy text. Best for lower-sensitivity workflows or internal reviews.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Confidential" icon="shield-halved">
        Screenshot capture is disabled and copy-paste is restricted at the viewer level. Designed for highly sensitive deals where information leakage is a primary concern.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Data Room">
    Click the **Create Data Room** button to finalize setup. ArgoMedix VDR provisions your new room instantly and redirects you to its home screen, where you can begin uploading documents and configuring access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a codename for sensitive deals (e.g., "Project Falcon") to avoid revealing deal details to reviewers before NDAs are signed.
</Tip>

## Data Room Settings

After creating a data room, you can fine-tune its behavior at any time by clicking the **gear icon** (⚙) in the data room header to open the Settings panel. Settings are organized into three tabs:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="General">
    Update the data room's **name** and **description**. Changes take effect immediately and are reflected in audit logs with a timestamp and the editor's name.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Security">
    Configure protective controls that apply to all reviewers in the room:

    - **Watermarking** — overlay each viewed page with the reviewer's email address and access timestamp to deter unauthorized sharing.
    - **Download restrictions** — choose whether reviewers can download files, view only in-browser, or access printable versions.
    - **Session timeout** — set an inactivity period (5–60 minutes) after which reviewer sessions are automatically terminated and require re-authentication.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Notifications">
    Set up **email alerts** to keep your team informed of data room activity, including:

    - New reviewer logins
    - Document views and downloads
    - Q&A submissions (if the Q&A module is enabled)
    - Access requests from uninvited users
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Archiving and Deleting Data Rooms

When a deal concludes or a review period ends, you have two options for closing out a data room: **archiving** or **deleting**.

**Archiving** transitions a data room to read-only status. Archived rooms remain fully accessible to workspace admins for compliance and record-keeping purposes, and all documents, audit logs, and activity history are preserved. No new uploads, permission changes, or reviewer invitations can be made to an archived room. To archive a room, open **Settings → Archive** and confirm your selection.

**Deleting** permanently removes the data room and all of its contents, including documents, reviewer lists, and audit logs. Deletion requires the **Admin** role and cannot be reversed by ArgoMedix VDR support.

<Warning>
  Deleting a data room is permanent and cannot be undone. Download all documents and audit reports before deleting.
</Warning>
