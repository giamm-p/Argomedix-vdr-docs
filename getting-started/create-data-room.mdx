When you first sign up for Argomedix VDR, the onboarding flow helps you configure a data room that matches your company stage and product type. You can accept a tailored folder structure with placeholder documents, start from scratch, or skip the MedTech path entirely. After setup, an interactive guided tour walks you through the core features.

## Choose your industry

The first step asks you to select your industry:

- **MedTech** - Access 32 data room templates curated by MedTech experts. The system asks follow-up questions to recommend the best structure.
- **Other industry** - Use a universal template for software and hardware companies.

## MedTech diagnostic profiling

If you choose MedTech, answer three questions so the system can suggest the right folder structure.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your company name">
    This becomes your workspace and data room name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your company stage">
    Choose from **Pre-seed**, **Seed**, **Series A**, or **Series B+**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your product type">
    Choose from:

    - **Non-invasive hardware** (wearable devices or instruments)
    - **Software/AI** (AI, digital health, or diagnostic software)
    - **Implantable device** (implants or other high-risk devices)
    - **Diagnostics** (in-vitro diagnostic kits or lab assays)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your regulatory data needs">
    Choose **Lab and bench testing** or **Human clinical trials**. If you selected **Implantable device**, this is automatically set to human clinical trials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Resulting profiles

Your answers map to one of 14 taxonomy profiles (TAX-01 through TAX-14). The profile determines which specialized folders are added to your data room. For example:

- Early-stage hardware with low clinical data needs (TAX-01) adds **Regulatory & Quality** and **Engineering & Product** folders focused on pre-submission strategy and design controls.
- Late-stage software with high clinical data needs (TAX-11) adds **Clinical & Scientific**, **Cloud Operations & Release Management**, and expanded **Cybersecurity & Data Privacy** sections.
- Class III implantable devices (TAX-05, TAX-09, TAX-12) always include clinical trial documentation, biocompatibility studies, and manufacturing readiness folders.

## Review and confirm your folder structure

After answering the questions, the system shows a **Recommended data room structure** preview. You can expand and collapse folders to inspect the full hierarchy. The preview displays:

- The total number of folders and placeholder files
- Your setup tags (for example, "Early stage", "Software/AI", "Clinical Studies")
- A tree view of every folder and subfolder

<Note>
  Placeholder documents are labeled as such. They were created by MedTech experts, not AI. You can replace them by uploading your own files, or rename and delete any you do not need.
</Note>

You have two options:

- **Use this folder structure** - Seeds your workspace with the full template.
- **Start with a blank data room** - Creates an empty workspace with no folders.

## Non-MedTech path

If you select **Other industry**, the flow is shorter:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your company name">
    This becomes your workspace name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the universal template">
    The generic taxonomy includes standard folders for corporate, financial, cap table, team, IP, market, and technology documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose template or blank">
    Accept the universal structure or start empty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common top-level folders

Whether you choose MedTech or another industry, your data room typically starts with these universal folders:

- **Executive Summary** - Pitch deck, one-pager, and high-level financial plan
- **Corporate & Legal** - Incorporation, governance, material contracts, compliance, and insurance
- **Financials** - Historical financials, projections, tax, and audit documents
- **Cap Table & Equity** - Current cap table, financing documents, and option pool details
- **Team** - Founder and executive agreements, employee documentation, and organizational chart
- **Intellectual Property** - Patents, trademarks, IP assignments, and strategy
- **Market & Commercial** - Market research, competitor analysis, and commercial strategy

### MedTech-specific folders

Depending on your diagnostic profile, the system may also add:

- **Regulatory & Quality** - FDA or MDR strategy, quality management system documentation, and post-market surveillance
- **Engineering & Product** - Design controls, verification and validation, software lifecycle, and cybersecurity
- **Clinical & Scientific** - Pre-clinical data, clinical investigation plans, and study reports
- **Manufacturing & Supply Chain** - Process validation, supplier quality agreements, and batch records
- **Cloud Operations & Release Management** - CI/CD pipelines, MLOps, and cloud hosting architecture (for SaMD profiles)

## Guided tutorial

After your data room is created, an interactive guided tour starts automatically on desktop. The tour covers:

1. **Data Room** - How to create folders, upload files, and organize content
2. **Permissions** - How to create groups and set granular folder and file permissions
3. **Team** - How to invite internal collaborators
4. **Share** - How to generate encrypted, trackable share links with expiration options
5. **Analytics** - How to track investor engagement and access compliance logs

The tour routes you between the Data Room, Permissions, and Analytics pages automatically. You can navigate forward and backward through the steps, or quit at any time.

### Restart the tutorial

You can restart the guided tour at any time from the top navigation bar:

1. Click the **Help** icon (question mark) in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Tutorial** from the dropdown menu.

<Warning>
  The interactive tutorial is optimized for desktop viewports. On mobile devices, you receive a notification that you can explore the data room using the navigation tabs and menu instead.
</Warning>

The system remembers whether you have completed the tutorial. Restarting it clears that status and begins the tour from the first step.
