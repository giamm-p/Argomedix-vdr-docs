Argomedix VDR is a secure data room built specifically for MedTech startups running fundraising due diligence. It gives you one workspace to organize documents, share selectively with investors, and track who is viewing what. New users sign up at [app.argomedix.com/welcome](https://app.argomedix.com/welcome), and existing users sign in at [app.argomedix.com/login](https://app.argomedix.com/login).

## What you can do

- **Organize with MedTech taxonomies**: Onboarding asks whether you are a MedTech startup, runs a diagnostic profile, and seeds your workspace with folders and placeholder documents tailored to your stage and product type.
- **Control access precisely**: Create investor groups, set per-folder and per-document permissions (hidden, view, or download), and toggle watermarks per item.
- **Share via secure links**: Generate shareable links tied to a group, optionally set an expiry date, and deactivate links at any time.
- **Track everything**: See total views, average session duration, file downloads, top guests, and a full audit log. Export activity reports to CSV.
- **Manage your team**: One workspace equals one data room. The workspace has exactly one owner (transferable) and can include admins. The Seed tier includes 1 GB storage and 1 admin seat; the Scale tier includes 10 GB and up to 10 admins.
- **View documents securely**: Built-in secure viewers for PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, images, video, DICOM, and CAD files, plus OCR-powered full-text search.
