ArgoMedix VDR is a secure virtual data room (VDR) platform built for the high-stakes document workflows of deal teams, investors, legal counsel, and life sciences or healthcare organizations. Whether you're managing due diligence for an M&A transaction, sharing clinical data with potential partners, or coordinating a fundraising round, ArgoMedix VDR gives you a centralized, audited, and permission-controlled environment to share sensitive materials with confidence. This documentation covers everything from setting up your first data room to configuring advanced access controls, tracking reviewer activity, and maintaining a complete audit trail.

## What You Can Do with ArgoMedix VDR

ArgoMedix VDR provides a full suite of tools to manage, protect, and analyze your sensitive documents throughout the deal lifecycle.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Secure Storage" icon="lock">
    Store all deal documents in an encrypted, access-controlled environment with enterprise-grade security.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Granular Permissions" icon="users">
    Assign precise access levels to individual users or groups — down to the folder and file level.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Document Analytics" icon="chart-bar">
    See exactly which documents reviewers opened, how long they spent on each page, and when they last accessed the room.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audit Trail" icon="clipboard-list">
    Maintain a tamper-evident log of every action taken in the data room for compliance and legal defensibility.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Version Control" icon="code-branch">
    Upload new versions of documents without breaking existing links, and retain the full history of every file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dynamic Watermarking" icon="droplet">
    Automatically apply viewer-specific watermarks to downloaded documents to deter unauthorized sharing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  ArgoMedix VDR is designed for life sciences, healthcare, and financial deal workflows where document security and compliance are paramount.
</Note>
