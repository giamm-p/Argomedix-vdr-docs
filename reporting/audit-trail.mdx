The audit trail is a tamper-evident log designed for compliance, legal discovery, and regulatory review — it captures every action with cryptographic verification so records cannot be altered after the fact. Unlike the Activity Log, which is optimized for day-to-day monitoring, the audit trail is a sealed, verifiable record intended for use in formal proceedings, regulatory submissions, and long-term retention.

## What the Audit Trail Records

The audit trail captures all events recorded in the Activity Log, and extends that record with the following additional fields required for compliance and legal use:

- **User IP addresses and geolocation** — the network origin of every session
- **Browser and OS details** — the client environment used to access the data room
- **Session start and end times** — the exact duration of each login session
- **NDA acceptance records** — full legal timestamps and user identity details for every NDA signed
- **Admin-level actions** — a complete log of all permission changes, settings updates, and administrative operations performed by data room owners and admins

Every entry in the audit trail is written once and cryptographically sealed, ensuring that no action — including those taken by ArgoMedix VDR administrators — can modify a record after it is created.

## Downloading the Audit Trail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Audit Trail Settings">
    Open the data room and click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then select **Audit Trail** from the settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Date Range">
    Use the date range picker to select the period you want to export, or choose **All Time** to download the complete audit trail for the data room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download Audit Trail">
    Click the **Download Audit Trail** button to initiate the export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Format">
    Select your preferred download format:
    - **PDF** — A digitally signed, tamper-evident report formatted for legal and regulatory use.
    - **CSV** — Raw event data in spreadsheet format for analysis or import into compliance tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Audit Trail Integrity

PDF audit trail downloads are digitally signed by ArgoMedix VDR at the moment of generation. Each downloaded file includes a unique **verification code** embedded in the document footer.

To confirm the authenticity of any audit trail PDF, visit **[app.argomedix.com/verify](https://app.argomedix.com/verify)** and enter the verification code. The system will confirm whether the document is genuine and unmodified, and display the original generation timestamp.

This verification mechanism means that any third party — a regulator, auditor, or court — can independently confirm that the audit trail you provide is the original, unaltered record produced by ArgoMedix VDR.

<Note>
  Audit trail records are retained for 7 years by default, in accordance with standard compliance requirements for due diligence and clinical data workflows.
</Note>

## Using the Audit Trail for Compliance

The ArgoMedix VDR audit trail is designed to satisfy the evidentiary and documentation requirements across a range of regulated workflows:

- **SEC/FINRA review** — Demonstrate controlled document access and investor communications during a financial deal audit.
- **FDA regulatory submissions** — Provide a verifiable record of clinical trial data sharing and access to investigational documents.
- **GDPR data access records** — Document who accessed what personal data, when, and from where to satisfy data subject access and accountability obligations.
- **Litigation and legal discovery** — Produce a court-ready record of all data room activity, including who reviewed which documents and when.

<Info>
  For legal proceedings, always use the signed PDF format — it includes the cryptographic signature chain required for court admissibility.
</Info>
