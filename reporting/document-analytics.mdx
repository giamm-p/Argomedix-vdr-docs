Document analytics go beyond simple view counts — ArgoMedix VDR tracks time spent per document, page-by-page engagement, and reviewer interaction patterns to help you understand deal interest. Whether you're managing a fundraising round, an M&A process, or a clinical data disclosure, these insights help you identify which investors or counterparties are engaging seriously and where their attention is focused.

## Accessing Document Analytics

From the data room, click **Analytics** in the left sidebar to open the Analytics dashboard. The dashboard opens to an overview showing:

- **Total Views** — the cumulative number of document opens across all users
- **Total Time Spent** — aggregate viewing time across all documents and all reviewers
- **Most-Viewed Documents** — a ranked list of the documents receiving the highest traffic

## Document-Level Metrics

Click any document name in the Analytics dashboard to open its detail view. This view surfaces the following metrics for that specific document:

- **Total Views** — how many times the document has been opened
- **Unique Viewers** — the number of distinct users who have opened it
- **Total Time Spent** — cumulative viewing time across all viewers
- **Average Time Per View** — total time divided by total views, indicating typical engagement depth
- **Page Heatmap** — a visual breakdown showing which pages received the most attention from reviewers

## Reviewer-Level Metrics

Click any reviewer's name in the **Users** tab of the Analytics dashboard to open their individual engagement summary. This view shows:

- Documents viewed by that reviewer
- Time spent on each document
- Last activity timestamp, indicating when they most recently accessed the data room

Use reviewer-level data to identify your most active prospects and tailor your follow-up accordingly.

## Page Heatmap

The Page Heatmap gives you a page-by-page visualization of where reviewers focused their attention within a document. Pages that received the most engagement are highlighted in **orange and red**, while less-viewed pages appear in **blue**. Pages with no recorded views remain uncolored.

Use the heatmap to quickly identify which sections of a pitch deck, term sheet, or clinical summary drove the most scrutiny — and which sections reviewers may have skipped entirely.

<Tip>
  Spikes in time spent on financial or IP sections often signal a reviewer is conducting serious due diligence. Use this insight to prioritize follow-up conversations.
</Tip>

## Exporting Analytics Reports

To export analytics data, click **Export Report** in the Analytics dashboard. Choose from three report formats:

- **Summary PDF** — An executive overview of data room engagement, suitable for sharing with deal leads or board members.
- **Detailed CSV** — All raw engagement events in a spreadsheet format, ideal for custom analysis or importing into BI tools.
- **Reviewer Report** — A per-user breakdown of activity, time spent, and documents accessed for each individual reviewer.

Reports can also be scheduled for **automatic weekly email delivery** — click **Schedule Report** after selecting your format to configure the recipients and delivery cadence.
