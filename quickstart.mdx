---

This guide walks you through everything you need to get ArgoMedix VDR working for your first deal — from creating your account to uploading documents and inviting a reviewer to your data room. By the end, you'll have a live, permission-controlled data room ready to share with collaborators, and you'll know how to track their activity from the moment they log in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up for ArgoMedix VDR">
    Navigate to [app.argomedix.com](https://app.argomedix.com) and click **Sign Up** in the top-right corner. Enter your full name, work email address, and a strong password, then click **Create Account**. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account and access your workspace dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your First Data Room">
    From the Dashboard, click **+ New Data Room** in the upper-left panel. Give your room a clear, descriptive name — for example, *Series B Due Diligence* — and add an optional description so collaborators understand its purpose. When you're ready, click **Create** to provision your data room and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Your First Documents">
    Inside your data room, click **Upload** in the toolbar. Drag and drop files directly onto the upload area, or click **Browse Files** to select them from your computer. ArgoMedix VDR supports the following formats:

    | Format | Extension |
    |--------|-----------|
    | PDF | `.pdf` |
    | Word Document | `.docx` |
    | Excel Spreadsheet | `.xlsx` |
    | PowerPoint Presentation | `.pptx` |
    | Images | `.png`, `.jpg` |

    The maximum file size is **500 MB per file**. Upload progress is shown in real time, and files appear in the data room as soon as processing is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a Reviewer">
    Click **Invite** in the top-right corner of your data room. Enter the reviewer's email address, then select the role that matches the level of access you want to grant:

    - **Viewer** — can read and download documents only
    - **Commenter** — can read, download, and leave comments on documents
    - **Editor** — can read, download, comment, and upload or modify files

    Click **Send Invitation**. Your reviewer will receive an email with a secure link to access the data room under the permissions you assigned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Document Activity">
    Click **Activity** in the left sidebar to open the activity log. Here you can see who has viewed each document, how long they spent reading it, which pages they focused on, and what they downloaded — giving you real-time insight into reviewer engagement throughout your deal process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Pro tip:** Organize your documents into labeled folders before inviting reviewers — it makes navigation much easier for guests who are unfamiliar with your file structure.
</Tip>
