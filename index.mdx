## Get Up and Running in 4 Steps

Follow these steps to go from a new account to a fully configured data room with active collaborators.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account" href="/getting-started/account-setup">
    Sign up at app.argomedix.com and verify your email to activate your ArgoMedix VDR workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Data Room" href="/data-room/overview">
    Create a named data room for your deal or project and configure its structure with folders and access policies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Documents" href="/data-room/uploading-documents">
    Drag and drop your deal files into the data room — ArgoMedix VDR supports PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Collaborators" href="/access/roles-and-permissions">
    Send role-based invitations to reviewers, investors, or legal counsel so they can access only what they need.
  </Step>
</Steps>
