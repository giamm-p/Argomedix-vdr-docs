Argomedix VDR notifies you about guest and team activity through an in-app bell and email digests. You control which events trigger alerts and how often you receive them.

## In-app notification bell

The notification bell in the top navigation shows a red badge with your unread count. Click it to open a dropdown of recent alerts. Notifications are automatically marked as read after you view them for one second. Click **Mark all as read** to clear your inbox.

The bell shows up to the 50 most recent notifications per workspace.

## Notification types you can receive

When email notifications are enabled, you can choose to be notified about:

- **New Guest Registration** - when an invited investor or guest registers their account
- **Guest Log In** - when a guest signs into the data room
- **Guest Viewed Document** - when a document or folder is viewed
- **Guest Downloaded Document** - when a document or file archive is downloaded

## Choose your digest frequency

1. Open **Account Settings** and select the **Notifications** tab.
2. Under **Frequency**, select one of the following:
   - **Immediately (Real-time emails)** - sent as events happen
   - **Hourly Digest** - grouped into an hourly summary
   - **Daily Digest** - sent once per day at your chosen time
   - **Weekly Digest** - sent once per week on your chosen day and time
   - **Disabled (No emails)** - no email notifications

If you select **Daily**, choose the time of day. If you select **Weekly**, choose the day of the week and the time. Your browser timezone is detected automatically and displayed next to the schedule.

## Storage quota alerts

Argomedix sends automatic in-app notifications when your workspace reaches storage milestones:

- **80% used** - "Data Quota Reached 80%"
- **90% used** - "Data Quota Reached 90%"

These alerts appear in your notification bell regardless of your email settings. Upgrade to Scale in **Billing** to increase your quota.
