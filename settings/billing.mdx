Manage everything related to your ArgoMedix VDR subscription from the Billing section — view your current plan and usage, update payment details, download past invoices, and change your plan as your deal volume grows. All billing actions are available in one place so your team can maintain uninterrupted access to your data rooms.

## Accessing Billing

To open the billing dashboard, click your **organization name** in the top-left dropdown and navigate to **Workspace Settings → Billing**. Only users with the **Organization Admin** role can view or make changes to billing settings. If you need access to an invoice or plan information, ask your organization's Admin to retrieve it on your behalf.

## Upgrading Your Plan

You can upgrade your ArgoMedix VDR plan at any time. The upgrade takes effect immediately and a prorated charge is applied for the remainder of your current billing period.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Change Plan screen">
    Navigate to **Workspace Settings → Billing → Change Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your new plan">
    Review the available plans and click **Select** under the tier you want to upgrade to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the prorated charge">
    ArgoMedix VDR will display the prorated amount due today based on the days remaining in your current billing period. Confirm the amount before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter or confirm your payment method">
    Select an existing payment method on file or add a new card. Verify the billing address matches your card details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the upgrade">
    Click **Confirm Upgrade**. Your new plan limits and features are activated instantly. You will receive an email confirmation with the charge details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  To **downgrade** your plan, contact **support@argomedix.com**. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period. If your current usage exceeds the limits of the lower tier, you will need to archive or delete data rooms and remove users before the downgrade is processed.
</Info>

## Managing Payment Methods

ArgoMedix VDR accepts the following payment methods:

- **Visa**
- **Mastercard**
- **American Express**
- **Wire transfer** *(Enterprise plan only)*

To view or update your payment methods, navigate to **Workspace Settings → Billing → Payment Methods**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a new card">
    Click **Add Payment Method**, enter your card number, expiration date, CVV, and billing address, then click **Save Card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as default">
    After saving, click **Set as Default** next to the new card. The default payment method is charged automatically at each billing renewal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove an old card">
    Click the **⋯** menu next to any non-default card and select **Remove**. You cannot remove the card currently set as your default without first designating another as default.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invoices

Every invoice generated for your ArgoMedix VDR subscription is stored permanently in your billing history and is available to download at any time.

To access your invoices, navigate to **Workspace Settings → Billing → Invoice History**. Click any invoice row to open it, then click **Download PDF** to save a copy. Invoices are also automatically emailed to the **billing contact** on the account at the time each charge is processed.

<Tip>
  To update who receives invoice emails, go to **Workspace Settings → Billing** and update the **Billing Contact Email** field. This does not have to be the same email address as the Organization Admin.
</Tip>

<Note>
  To cancel your subscription, contact **support@argomedix.com**. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. Your data is retained for **30 days** after cancellation before being permanently deleted — export any documents or audit logs you need to preserve before that window closes.
</Note>
