Argomedix VDR offers two subscription tiers designed for MedTech startups at different stages of fundraising. You can upgrade or downgrade directly from the **Billing** tab in **Workspace Settings**.

## Tier comparison

| Feature | Seed | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storage | 1 GB | 10 GB |
| Workspace admins | 1 (Owner only) | Up to 10 |
| Guests | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Data rooms | 1 | 1 |

## View your current usage

The **Usage Meters** in the top navigation and the **Billing** tab show two progress bars:

- **Storage** - current usage against your tier limit in GB
- **Admins** - current active admin count against your seat limit

Bars turn amber above 80% and red above 90%. Click either meter to jump to the relevant settings section.

## Upgrade to Scale

1. Open **Workspace Settings** and select the **Billing** tab.
2. Click **Upgrade to Scale**.
3. Review the features in the modal: 10 GB storage, up to 10 admins, and unlimited guests.
4. Click **Upgrade Now**.

The change takes effect immediately. Only the Owner can upgrade.

## Downgrade to Seed

You can downgrade from Scale to Seed if your usage fits within Seed limits.

1. In the **Billing** tab, click **Downgrade to Seed**.
2. Argomedix checks whether you exceed Seed limits.

If you have more than 1 admin or more than 1 GB of storage, a modal lists the issues you must resolve first:

- **Seat Limit Exceeded** - remove admins until only the Owner remains
- **Storage Limit Exceeded** - delete files until you are under 1 GB

Once eligible, click **Downgrade to Seed** again to complete the change.
