The Account Settings modal in Argomedix VDR contains your profile, notification preferences, security controls, and appearance options. Open it from the top navigation to manage your account.

## My Profile

### Update Your Profile Details

1. Go to the **My Profile** tab.
2. Edit your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Title**, **Company**, and **Phone Number**.
3. Click **Save Profile**.

### Upload or Remove a Profile Photo

1. In the **My Profile** tab, find the **Profile Picture** section.
2. Click **Upload picture** (or **Replace picture**) and select a PNG, JPG, WEBP, or SVG file (max 2 MB).
3. Click **Remove** to delete your current photo.

### Change Your Email Address

Email changes are handled in the **Security** tab for verification safety. See the Email Change section below.

## Security Settings

### Change Your Password

1. Open Account Settings and go to the **Security** tab.
2. Enter your **Current Password**.
3. Enter and confirm your **New Password** (minimum 6 characters).
4. Click **Update Password**.

For your security, changing your password signs you out of all other devices and sends a security alert email with an account freeze link.

<Note>
  If you signed up with Google (or another OAuth provider), you cannot change your password directly. You must first convert to an email and password account.
</Note>

### Convert a Google Sign-In Account to Password

1. In the **Security** tab, click **Convert to Email/Password Account**.
2. Enter and confirm a new password (minimum 6 characters).
3. Click **Convert**.

This unlinks your Google identity, creates a local password, and prevents future logins via Google. After converting, you can change your email and password normally.

### Change Your Email Address

1. In the **Security** tab, enter your **New Email Address** and **Current Password**.
2. Click **Request Email Change**.
3. Check your **old** email inbox for a confirmation link and click it.
4. Check your **new** email inbox for a verification link and click it.

The change remains pending until both links are clicked. Once confirmed, you are signed out of all sessions and must log in with your new email address.

### Sign Out Everywhere

Changing your password or completing an email change automatically signs you out of all active sessions on every device. There is no separate button; it happens as part of those security actions.

### Freeze Your Account

If you receive a security alert email after a password change, it contains an **account freeze link**. Clicking it opens a confirmation page where you can immediately freeze your account. Freezing:

- Locks your account and disables login
- Terminates all active sessions globally
- Requires contacting [support@argomedix.com](mailto:support@argomedix.com) to unfreeze

## Auto Logout on Inactivity

Argomedix VDR automatically logs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity to protect your data room.

- At 25 minutes of inactivity, an **Inactivity Warning** modal appears with a 5-minute countdown.
- Click **Stay Logged In** to reset the timer and dismiss the modal.
- If you do nothing, you are logged out at 30 minutes and returned to the login page.

The timer pauses while files are uploading so you are not interrupted during large transfers.
