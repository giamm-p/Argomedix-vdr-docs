Argomedix VDR workspaces have two internal team roles: **Owner** and **Admin**. This page covers how to invite team members, manage pending invitations, remove members, leave a workspace, and transfer ownership.

## Team Roles

| Capability | Owner | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invite team members | Yes | No |
| Remove team members | Yes | No |
| Transfer ownership | Yes | No |
| Upgrade or downgrade tier | Yes | No |
| Empty data room | Yes | No |
| Schedule workspace deletion | Yes | No |
| Manage folders, documents, and guest permissions | Yes | Yes |
| View analytics and audit log | Yes | Yes |
| Leave workspace | No (must transfer first) | Yes |

There is exactly one owner per workspace. The owner can transfer ownership to any active admin.

## Invite a Team Member

1. Open **Workspace Settings** and go to the **Team** tab.
2. Under **Invite a Team Member**, enter the person's email address.
3. The role is fixed to **Admin**.
4. Click **Send Invite**.

The invitee receives an email with a link to join the workspace. The invitation expires after 24 hours.

<Note>
  Tier limits apply:

  - **Seed:** 1 admin total (including the owner). You cannot invite another admin without upgrading.
  - **Scale:** up to 10 admins total.
</Note>

If you hit a limit, an upgrade prompt appears automatically.

## Manage Pending Invitations

Pending invitations appear in the team list with an **Invitation Pending** or **Invitation Expired** status.

- **Resend:** Click **Resend** to generate a new 24-hour invitation link and re-send the email.
- **Revoke:** Click **Revoke** to cancel the invitation and remove the pending member record.

## Remove a Team Member

1. Find the active admin in the team list.
2. Click **Remove** next to their name.
3. Confirm in the dialog.

The member loses workspace access immediately. This action is logged in the audit trail.

## Leave a Workspace

Admins who are not the owner can leave a workspace:

1. Go to **Workspace Settings** > **General**.
2. In the Danger Zone section, click **Leave Workspace**.
3. Confirm to permanently revoke your own access.

Owners cannot leave until they transfer ownership to another admin.

## Transfer Ownership

1. In **Workspace Settings** > **General**, find **Transfer Ownership**.
2. Select an active admin from the dropdown.
3. Click **Transfer**.
4. Type the exact data room name to confirm.

After transfer, the previous owner becomes an admin and the new owner gains full owner privileges. The new owner receives an in-app notification that ownership has been transferred.
