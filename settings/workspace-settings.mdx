The Workspace Settings modal lets you control how your Argomedix VDR data room looks and behaves. Open it from the top navigation to rename your room, set a company logo, configure default sharing rules, and perform owner-only actions.

## Open Workspace Settings

1. Click your workspace name or the settings icon in the top navigation bar.
2. The modal opens with tabs: **General**, **Appearance**, **Billing**, **Team**, and **Customization**.

<Note>
  Some settings are read-only for admins. Only the workspace owner can change the data room name, logo, billing tier, and danger-zone actions.
</Note>

## General Settings

### Rename the Data Room

1. In the **General** tab, find the **Data Room Name** field.
2. Enter a new name and click **Save**.

### View Storage Usage

Your storage bar shows the exact megabytes used and the percentage of your tier quota.

- **Seed tier:** 1 GB storage
- **Scale tier:** 10 GB storage

The bar turns amber above 60% and red above 85%.

### Export Data Room Structure

Click **Export CSV** to download a spreadsheet index of every folder, subfolder, and document with its index order, size, upload date, and parent folder. This is useful for offline due diligence checklists.

### Leave the Workspace (Admin only)

Admins who are not the owner can leave the workspace at any time from the General tab. Owners must transfer ownership first before leaving.

## Appearance Settings

### Upload a Company Logo

1. Go to the **Appearance** tab.
2. Click **Upload** and select a PNG, JPG, SVG, or WEBP file (max 2 MB).
3. Check the **Display your company logo on the header** checkbox to make it visible to all visitors.
4. Click **Remove** to delete the logo and hide it.

## Customization Settings

### Default Visibility Permission

Choose what happens when an admin uploads or moves a folder or document:

- **Keep current settings (Default):** Retains the item's existing explicit permissions. When copying or moving, it keeps the source permissions.
- **Inherit destination settings:** Removes any explicit overrides so the item follows the destination folder's permissions.
- **Set to hidden:** Explicitly hides the item from all guest groups by default.

### Default Watermark on Files

Toggle whether newly uploaded files are watermarked by default:

- **Watermarked Files**
- **Original files (No Watermark - Default)**

### Show Empty Folders

Enable the checkbox to display empty folders in the data room and on the Permissions page. This is useful when you are building out the taxonomy before uploading documents.

## Billing Settings

### Current Plan

The **Billing** tab shows your active tier:

- **Seed:** 1 GB storage, 1 workspace admin
- **Scale:** 10 GB storage, up to 10 workspace admins, unlimited guests

### Upgrade to Scale

If you are on Seed, click **Upgrade to Scale**. The upgrade takes effect immediately.

### Downgrade to Seed

If you are on Scale, click **Downgrade to Seed**. You can only downgrade if you are within Seed limits:

- 1 admin or fewer (including the owner)
- 1 GB of storage or less

If you exceed either limit, a modal lists exactly how many admins you must remove or how much storage you must free before downgrading.

## Danger Zone (Owner Only)

These actions appear in the General tab and are restricted to the workspace owner.

### Remove Placeholders

Click **Remove Placeholders** to permanently delete all placeholder files and empty placeholder folders. Folders that contain uploaded documents are preserved.

### Transfer Ownership

1. Select an active admin from the dropdown.
2. Click **Transfer**.
3. Confirm by typing the data room name.

You will lose owner privileges and become an admin. Only the new owner can manage billing, empty the data room, or schedule deletion.

### Empty Data Room

Click **Delete Data Room** to permanently remove every document, folder, and share link. Before confirming, you can check an option to **Revoke access for all external Guests**, which deactivates every guest while keeping their activity in the audit log.

### Schedule Deletion

Click **Schedule Deletion** to start a 7-day countdown. At the end of the countdown, the workspace and all its contents are permanently deleted. While the countdown is active, the General tab shows the scheduled date and offers a **Cancel Deletion** button to stop it.
