Workspace settings control the global configuration for your organization's ArgoMedix VDR account. Any changes you make here apply across all data rooms unless a specific data room overrides them at the room level — making this the right place to establish organization-wide defaults, branding, and security policies before you begin creating rooms.

## Accessing Workspace Settings

To open workspace settings, click your **organization name** in the top-left dropdown menu and select **Workspace Settings**. Only users with the **Admin** role can view or modify workspace settings. If you do not see this option, contact your organization's Admin to request elevated access.

## General Settings

The **General** tab lets you configure the foundational details of your ArgoMedix VDR organization account.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Organization Name** | The display name shown across the platform and in outbound communications. |
| **Organization Logo** | Uploaded logo appears in email invitations and the reviewer portal. Recommended size: 200×60 px, PNG or SVG. |
| **Default Timezone** | Sets the timezone used for activity timestamps and scheduled events. |
| **Default Language** | Sets the language for the platform UI for new workspace members. |
| **Date Format** | Choose from MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, or YYYY-MM-DD. |

## Team Management

The **Team** tab gives you a central view of every member in your workspace and lets you manage access at the organization level.

<Steps>
  <Step title="View workspace members">
    Open **Workspace Settings → Team** to see all current members, their email addresses, assigned roles, and join dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a new member">
    Click **Invite Member**, enter the recipient's email address, assign a workspace role, then click **Send Invite**. The invitee receives an email with a secure join link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a member's role">
    Find the member in the list, click the role badge next to their name, and select a new role from the dropdown. Changes take effect immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a member">
    Click the **⋯** menu next to the member's name and select **Remove from Workspace**. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Removing a workspace member does not revoke their access to individual data rooms — revoke data room access separately from the data room's **Users** tab.
</Note>

## Single Sign-On (SSO)

Enterprise plan customers can configure SAML 2.0 SSO so that team members authenticate through your existing Identity Provider (IdP) — such as Okta, Azure AD, or Google Workspace — instead of managing a separate ArgoMedix VDR password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SSO configuration panel">
    Navigate to **Workspace Settings → Security → Single Sign-On**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your IdP metadata URL">
    Paste the metadata URL provided by your Identity Provider into the **IdP Metadata URL** field. ArgoMedix VDR will automatically parse your entity ID, SSO URL, and signing certificate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test Connection** to verify that ArgoMedix VDR can communicate with your IdP. Resolve any errors before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable SSO">
    Once the test passes, toggle **Enable SSO** to activate. All workspace members will be required to authenticate via your IdP on their next login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  SSO configuration requires an **Enterprise plan**. If you are on a Starter or Business plan, upgrade your subscription before attempting to configure SSO.
</Warning>

## Default Data Room Settings

The settings below apply automatically to every new data room you create. Individual data rooms can override any of these defaults in their own room-level settings.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Watermarking" icon="droplet">
    When enabled, ArgoMedix VDR overlays a dynamic watermark — containing the viewer's email address and timestamp — on all documents viewed in the browser. **Default: On.**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Allow Downloads" icon="arrow-down-to-line">
    Controls whether reviewers can download original files from a data room. Disable this to enforce view-only access across rooms. **Default: Off.**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Session Timeout" icon="clock">
    Automatically logs out inactive reviewer sessions. Choose from **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **4 hours**, or **Never**. **Default: 30 minutes.**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="NDA Required" icon="file-signature">
    When enabled, reviewers must accept a Non-Disclosure Agreement before they can access any documents in the room. **Default: Off.**
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
