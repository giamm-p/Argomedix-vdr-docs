## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do reviewers need to create an account?">
    No. Reviewers access your data room through a secure email invitation link. They do not need to create an ArgoMedix VDR account. If your room requires NDA acceptance, they complete that step before viewing documents.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many data rooms can I create?">
    The number of data rooms available depends on your subscription plan:

    - **Starter** — up to 3 data rooms
    - **Business** — unlimited data rooms
    - **Enterprise** — unlimited data rooms

    See the [Billing](/settings/billing) page for full plan details and a feature comparison.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use ArgoMedix VDR on mobile?">
    Yes. The reviewer portal and admin dashboard are fully responsive and work on iOS and Android browsers. A native mobile app is available on the App Store and Google Play for Business and Enterprise customers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security & Compliance

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    By default, your data is stored in our US data centers (AWS us-east-1). Business and Enterprise customers can choose EU or APAC data residency in **Workspace Settings → Data Residency**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is ArgoMedix VDR HIPAA compliant?">
    Yes. ArgoMedix VDR is HIPAA compliant for healthcare customers. Contact support to request a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for your organization.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
    Your data remains accessible until the end of your current billing period. After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days to allow export, then permanently deleted.

    <Warning>
      Download your documents and audit trail before cancelling. Data cannot be recovered after the 30-day retention window expires.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Document Management

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I restrict a reviewer from downloading documents?">
    Yes. In **Data Room Settings → Security**, toggle off **Allow Downloads**. You can also override this setting at the folder or document level for more granular control. Reviewers can still view documents in the secure browser viewer even when downloads are disabled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file types are supported?">
    ArgoMedix VDR supports the following file types:

    | Category | Formats |
    |---|---|
    | Documents | PDF, DOCX, DOC, TXT, RTF |
    | Spreadsheets | XLSX, XLS, CSV |
    | Presentations | PPTX, PPT |
    | Images | PNG, JPG, JPEG, GIF, TIFF |
    | Archives | ZIP (auto-extracted on upload) |

    See [Uploading Documents](/data-room/uploading-documents) for full details and size limits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does version control work?">
    Right-click any document and select **Upload New Version** to replace it with an updated file. The full version history is preserved and accessible to Admins. Reviewers always see the current version automatically — no action required on their part.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Access & Invitations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I resend an invitation?">
    To resend a pending invitation:

    1. Open your data room and go to the **Users** tab.
    2. Find the pending user in the list.
    3. Click the **⋯** menu next to their name.
    4. Select **Resend Invitation**.

    The user will receive a fresh invitation email with a new secure link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I set an expiry date on a user's access?">
    Yes. When inviting a user, set an **Access Expiry** date in the invitation form. Their access is automatically revoked at midnight on that date. You can extend access at any time by going to the **Users** tab, clicking **⋯** next to the user, and selecting **Edit Access**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I invite someone to multiple data rooms?">
    Yes. You can invite the same email address to as many data rooms as needed. Each invitation carries its own role and permissions, independent of any other rooms that person has been invited to.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
