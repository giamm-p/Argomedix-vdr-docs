Argomedix VDR offers several ways to get help and share feedback. You can email the support team directly, report bugs and suggest features from inside the app, and stay current on product updates through the built-in changelog.

## Email support

For questions about your account, billing, or data room setup, email the support team at [support@argomedix.com](mailto:support@argomedix.com).

## In-app feedback

When you are inside a workspace, the footer at the bottom of the screen contains quick links to report bugs, request features, and view the changelog. These options appear on core application routes such as Data Room, Permissions, Analytics, Search, and Dashboard.

### Report a bug

Click the bug icon in the footer to open the **Report a Bug** modal. The form asks for:

- **Title**: a short summary of the bug (minimum 3 characters)
- **Priority**: choose from Low, Medium, High, or Critical
- **Description**: what you were trying to do and what happened instead (minimum 10 characters)
- **Attachments** (optional): images or PDFs up to 5 MB each

When you submit the report, the app automatically includes your current URL, browser, viewport size, and screen resolution. You will see a confirmation message when the report is sent successfully.

### Feature requests

Click the lightbulb icon in the footer to open the **Feature Requests** modal. You can:

- **Browse requests**: see features submitted by other users, sorted by development status and upvote count
- **Upvote**: click the upvote button next to a request to show your support
- **Submit a new request**: enter a title up to 100 characters and click **Request New Feature**

Each feature displays a status badge so you can see where it stands:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Under Review | The team is evaluating the request |
| Planned | The request is on the roadmap |
| In Progress | The feature is actively being built |
| Coming Soon | The feature is nearly ready for release |

<Note>
  You can submit up to 5 feature requests and up to 5 upvotes per day. If you reach the daily limit, the app will prompt you to try again the next day or contact [support@argomedix.com](mailto:support@argomedix.com).
</Note>

You can remove an upvote only if you cast it during the current session.

### What's new and version information

The footer also shows the current app version number. Click it to open the **What's New** modal, which displays a timeline of releases. Each entry lists the version number, release date, and the specific features or improvements included in that update.

If a new version has been released since you last checked, a **New Update!** badge appears in the footer. Click the badge or the version number to open the changelog and mark the update as seen. Your seen version is saved across devices when you are signed in.
