## Before Contacting Support

Trying these steps first often resolves issues faster than waiting for a support response:

- Review the [Troubleshooting guide](/support/troubleshooting) for step-by-step fixes to common problems
- Search this documentation using the search bar at the top of the page
- Check the live status page at [status.argomedix.com](https://status.argomedix.com) to confirm there are no ongoing incidents
- Try reproducing the issue in a different browser or an incognito/private window to rule out extension or cache conflicts

## What to Include in Your Support Request

<Info>
  Including the following details in your initial message helps the support team resolve your issue without back-and-forth:

  - **Account email** — the email address associated with your ArgoMedix VDR workspace
  - **Data room name** — if your issue is specific to a particular room
  - **Browser and operating system** — e.g., Chrome 124 on macOS 14
  - **Steps to reproduce** — a brief description of what you did before the issue occurred
  - **Error messages or screenshots** — attach any relevant visuals or paste error text directly into your message
</Info>

<Tip>
  Enterprise customers can request a dedicated onboarding call with a Customer Success Manager by emailing **success@argomedix.com**.
</Tip>
